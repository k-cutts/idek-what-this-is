## About Katie Cutts's CollectionBuilder Site

This website is for project 1a in SI 676 Managing Digital Collections. For this project I have collected a series of images from a group of free to use images from the Library of Congress website. The images I chose were all part of the Fish and Fishing collection. The link to the webpage featuring all of these images is https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use/fish-and-fishing/.
